# Engineering Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing a solution or project as a developer, mentor or interviewer.

## Correctness
- Does it satisfy the stated requirements?
- Are assumptions explicit?
- Are boundary cases handled?
- Are errors handled at the right abstraction level?

## Readability
- Do names communicate intent?
- Are methods focused?
- Is control flow easy to follow?
- Are comments explaining why rather than repeating what?

## Design
- Are responsibilities separated sensibly?
- Is state protected by clear invariants?
- Are abstractions justified by a real need?
- Are dependencies and side effects visible?

## Performance
- What is the time complexity?
- What is the space complexity?
- Is the data structure appropriate?
- Is optimisation supported by evidence rather than guesswork?

## Testing
- Is normal behaviour tested?
- Are boundary and failure cases tested?
- Are tests repeatable and independent?
- Do tests verify behaviour rather than implementation details?

## Communication
- Can the author explain the approach?
- Can they describe alternatives and trade-offs?
- Can they acknowledge limitations?
- Can they respond to a changed requirement?
